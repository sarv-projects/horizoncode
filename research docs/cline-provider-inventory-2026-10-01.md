## Scope and disposition

- HorizonCode has no Cline/OpenCode provider parity implied by this source inventory. Each source row needs a HorizonCode-owned adapter, authorized credential route, reviewed origin/protocol, terms/registration clearance, and route conformance before it can be usable.
- Cline uses apiKeyEnv as generated metadata and configuration data. Absence does not mean no-auth; presence does not prove a vendor-supported auth method. Unknown stays explicit.
- This pin fixes the generated Cline catalog files; live Models.dev data may drift. AX-363 must detect source/catalog drift and prepare a reviewable candidate without refreshing runtime auth or executing remote packages.
- This inventory is not a legal conclusion, a live-provider test, a full vendor-terms review, or evidence that HorizonCode can use a provider account, subscription, or OAuth client.
